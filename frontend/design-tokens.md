# Friday — graphite / jade

Friday brings a device, its manual, and a conversation into one place.
The landing uses an interactive 3D router connected to an example conversation.
The application is its working counterpart, with a calm transcript and source rail.

- Graphite #101715, navigation #0c1210, elevated #19231e.
- Text #f1f5ef, muted #a8b6ac, jade #b5e4bc with #132b1b text.
- Light landing sections #edf1e9, dark text #15231b, secondary #526157.
- Self-hosted Manrope variable; display 44–80px, section 32–56px,
  application title 24–32px, body 14–18px, metadata at least 12px.
- Space: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px.
- Radius: 8px controls, 16px surfaces, 24px demonstration frames.
- Directional shadows only for elevated objects.
- 180ms controls, 400ms transitions; pointer rotation and explicit exploded view.
- Full viewport app, independently scrolling transcript, panels become drawers.
- Hero is illustrative hardware, not a model-specific repair diagram.
- No invented statistics, testimonials, or live activity.

The user delegated the visual decision. Code-led implementation; design seed
cba44387 considered repair desk, hardware showroom, service manual, audio
console, optical bench, component catalog, and portable diagnostic instrument.
The seventh supplies the product-centered depth and control discipline.
